Film Vault — Sample image provenance

JustShoot created the 39 source illustrations with OpenAI ImageGen. People, activities and locations depict fictional scenes inspired by everyday settings in Japan, the United States and Britain.

The DNG masters preserve the generated 8-bit sRGB levels with a 16-bit linearization table and lossless JPEG compression. Each unfiltered in-app JPEG is an sRGB rendering of the corresponding DNG. The app applies the selected JustShoot LUT to that JPEG when displaying a film look.

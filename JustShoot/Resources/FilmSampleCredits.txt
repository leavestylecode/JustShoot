Film Vault — Sample image provenance

JustShoot created the 39 source illustrations with OpenAI ImageGen. People, activities and locations depict fictional scenes inspired by everyday settings in Japan, the United States and Britain.

The generated sRGB images were converted to 16-bit linear RGB DNG files. The source signal has 8-bit sRGB precision. Each unfiltered in-app JPEG is an sRGB rendering of the corresponding DNG. The comparison HEIC applies the selected JustShoot LUT to that same JPEG crop.

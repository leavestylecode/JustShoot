# Film Vault sample image prompts

OpenAI ImageGen generated one image per scene. Each generation used the shared instructions followed by its scene prompt.

## Shared instructions

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a single original horizontal 3:2 photograph (1536 × 1024). This is an ungraded source image for a camera app. Render like the neutral development of a modern iPhone main-camera RAW at 1× or a modest 1.3× crop. Use a 24–32 mm-equivalent mobile perspective from a natural viewing distance; for portraits step back for a natural face shape. Moderate-to-deep depth of field, no Portrait Mode cutout blur. Keep sky gradation, bright neutral fabrics, believable smooth highlight rolloff and open textured shadows. An ordinary camera rendering, no local HDR exaggeration, crushed blacks or over-sharpened edges. Neutral sRGB preview of the original scene, natural white balance appropriate to the actual light, modest saturation, true neutral greys, clean blacks, texture in highlights and shadows. Everyday observational color photography, calm precise composition, one clear subject with an intelligible setting, slightly off-centre visual balance, ordinary signs of use, credible scale and anatomy. Eye-level or natural observational viewpoint, sharp photographic detail with enough depth of field to read the place. No filter, film grain, retro wash, cinematic colour grade, HDR halos, tilt shift, ornamental objects, text overlays, watermarks, collage, frames or added graphic elements. Fill the entire image with the scene.
